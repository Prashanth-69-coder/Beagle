The Beagle Blog

The Beagle is a small to medium-sized breed of dog that is known for its friendly and curious nature. They are often used as hunting dogs due to their excellent sense of smell and tracking abilities.

Trivia

  1. Height - 13–16 in (33–41 cm)
  2. Weight - 22–25 lb (10.0–11.3 kg)
  3. Coat -Short haired, hard coat of medium length
  4. Colour -Tricolor or white in combination with black & tan/brown or brown/tan
  5. Life span - 12–15 years
